## 📖 Your OOP Exercise: Library Shelf!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a library shelf containing books. Each book has a title, an author, and a flag showing whether it is available. Print the books and allow someone to borrow a book by title.

A book can only be borrowed if it exists on the shelf and is available. In `App`, try borrowing an available book, borrowing it again, and borrowing a book that isn't on the shelf.

### 💡 Bonus

Add a method for returning a borrowed book.
