## 📚 Your OOP Exercise: Books & Authors!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a `Book` class and an `Author` class. Each book has a title and an author. Give the book a method that prints its title and its author's name.

In an `App` class, create an author and a book, connect them, and print the book's details. Then change the author's name and print the book again.

### 💡 Bonus

Create a second book by the same author. What happens to both books when you change the author's name?
