## 🎵 Your OOP Exercise: Playlist!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create `Song` and `Playlist` classes. A playlist has a name and a list of songs. Add songs to a playlist and print all their titles and artists.

In `App`, try one playlist with several songs and another with no songs.

### 💡 Bonus

Give `Song` a method that prints a pretend "Now playing" message.
