## 🐾 Your OOP Exercise: Pets & Owners!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a `Pet` and an `Owner`. Each pet has a name, an age, and an owner. Give the pet a method to introduce itself and another method to celebrate its birthday.

In `App`, create two pets that share an owner. Introduce them, celebrate one pet's birthday, and introduce them again.

### 💡 Bonus

Change the owner's name. Do both pets use the new name?
