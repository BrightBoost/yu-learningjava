## ☕ Your OOP Exercise: Coffee Shop Order!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a `Drink` and an `Order`. An order has a customer name, one drink, and a quantity. Let the order print a summary and calculate its total price.

Create two orders in `App` and compare their totals.

### 💡 Bonus

Have both orders use the same drink. Change the drink's price and see what happens to their totals.
