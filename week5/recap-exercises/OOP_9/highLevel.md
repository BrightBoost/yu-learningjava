## 💅 Your OOP Exercise: Beauty Salon!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Build a small beauty salon that keeps a list of appointments. Each appointment connects a customer with a service, such as a haircut or manicure.

The salon should print all appointments and calculate the total price of the booked services. In `App`, create a salon and try it with several appointments.

### 💡 Bonus

Print only the appointments for a particular customer.
