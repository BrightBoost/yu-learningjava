## ✨ Your OOP Exercise: Beauty Salon Booking System!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Build a salon with customers, stylists, services, and appointments. Each stylist has a list of appointments. The salon has a list of stylists.

A stylist cannot accept two appointments at the same time. Print each stylist's schedule and calculate the value of all accepted bookings.

In `App`, test a conflicting booking for one stylist and a booking at the same time with a different stylist.

### 💡 Bonus

Add a method to cancel an appointment and check that its time becomes available again.
