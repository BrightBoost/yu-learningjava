## 🛒 Your OOP Exercise: Shopping Basket!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a shopping basket containing a list of products. Each product has a name and a price. The basket should print its contents and calculate the total cost.

In `App`, test it with several products and with an empty basket.

### 💡 Bonus

Add the same product object twice. Is its price counted once or twice?
