## ⚔️ Your OOP Exercise: Adventure Game!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a small game where a player carries a list of items. Each item has a name and a healing value. The player can pick up items, view their inventory, and use an item to restore health.

Using an item removes it from the inventory. Health cannot rise above the player's maximum health. In `App`, try healing the player and using an item that is not in the inventory.

### 💡 Bonus

Give some items a healing value of `0` and describe what they might be used for in a future version of the game.
