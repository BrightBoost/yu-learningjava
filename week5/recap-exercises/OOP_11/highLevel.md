## 🏫 Your OOP Exercise: School!

### High-level Instructions (for confident coders)

Create a school with courses, teachers, and students. Each course has one teacher and a list of enrolled students. The school has a list of courses.

In `App`, print the school's courses, including their teachers and students. Also calculate how many student enrollments the school has in total.

### 💡 Bonus

Let a student enroll in more than one course. How does that affect the total?
